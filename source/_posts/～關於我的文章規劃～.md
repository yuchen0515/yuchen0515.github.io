   > 這個欠超久了...之前都只有簡單寫 FB、Linkedin，我從中真的獲得很多也非常開心，但反而使我覺得不管怎樣寫都不夠好，結果就一直拖延「正式」實習心得文了QQ

- **字幕生成系統是怎麼煉成的**
   > 關於這個是從我跟同學在台大 DSP 課程期末專題誕生的產物，起初希望可以偷懶，把上課影片都扔進去跑一次，直接讀字幕快速上課。但之後發現使用的 WeNet Toolkit 無法應付中英混雜，加上教授授課都習慣中英混雜。近期串接 Whisper 模型，正確率高得嚇人，包括老師說「老師把毛衣脫起來一下」都正確辨識無誤，中文、英文都辨識的很好，最後也拿來輔助我們期中考準備。之後想做一個 side project，規劃字幕生成系統的前後端，同時因為 Whisper 是需要一定程度的算力的，希望可以規劃一個系統可以控制流量XD 而我相信也會是一個很好用的工具

## About This Random Post (X)

Hello everyone! I usually enjoy sharing and discussing technology with others, but due to my high standards for articles and being constantly busy... I always hope to write detailed articles like LeeMing's, but procrastination keeps happening again and again. I'm hopeless...

 ![](../images/2023-05-11-23-19-40.png)

Here are several possibilities for future article planning:

- **The Smooth Journey with Neovim**
   > Over the past two months, I've switched from vim to neovim, and it feels like the enlightenment of switching from Windows to Linux (X). This could include how to start from scratch and use neovim smoothly, or organize common shortcuts, and also include my recent research on the smooth combination of LaTeX + neovim, which feels like having Overleaf locally - all these are topics worth discussing.

- **KKBOX Internship Sharing**
   > I've owed this for so long... Previously, I only wrote brief posts on FB and LinkedIn. I really gained a lot and was very happy with the experience, but this made me feel that no matter how I write, it's never good enough, so I kept delaying the "official" internship reflection post QQ

- **How the Subtitle Generation System Was Built**
   > This originated from a final project I did with classmates in NTU's DSP course. Initially, we wanted to be lazy and throw all lecture videos into it to run once, then directly read subtitles for quick studying. But later we found that the WeNet Toolkit couldn't handle mixed Chinese-English content, and professors usually teach in mixed languages. Recently, integrating the Whisper model, the accuracy is surprisingly high, including correctly recognizing when the teacher said "Teacher, let me take off my sweater for a moment" - both Chinese and English are recognized very well, and it finally helped us prepare for midterm exams. I want to make this a side project, planning the frontend and backend of a subtitle generation system. Since Whisper requires a certain level of computing power, I hope to plan a system that can control traffic XD And I believe it will be a very useful tool.
